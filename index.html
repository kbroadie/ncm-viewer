How to read it

A tokenizer compresses the finished text into a stack of codes, coarse on top and fine at the bottom. The generator predicts that stack from the top down. Each level is one forward pass, and each level covers a longer sequence than the one before, which is why the bars grow.

Early levels hold the gist: frequent words, names, punctuation. Later levels sort out order and wording. An autoregressive model would spend one pass per token; here the whole output takes as many passes as there are levels.
